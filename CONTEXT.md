# Brasaland — Company Context

## Company Overview

Brasaland is a grilled food restaurant chain with 14 company-owned locations across Colombia and Florida, USA.

The company focuses on consistent food quality, warm customer experiences, and fast service.

## Project Background

Brasaland Digital is the company's internal digital transformation team.

Restaurant managers currently handle many operational tasks manually, including tracking sales, calculating margins, managing food waste, and ordering ingredients.

Felipe Guerrero, the Operations Director, needs a system that can automate these processes.

## Milestone 2: Programming Fundamentals

As a junior developer, my responsibility is to build reusable TypeScript data models and functions for:

- Managing menu items, sales, locations, and waste records
- Filtering, sorting, and searching operational data
- Calculating revenue, margins, and waste costs
- Comparing restaurant performance
- Validating business data
- Supporting both USD and COP currencies

## Core Data Models

- MenuItem: Restaurant menu items, prices, costs, and availability
- SaleTransaction: Sales transactions and payment information
- Location: Restaurant locations and operational information
- WasteRecord: Ingredient waste and associated costs
- CountryMetrics: Aggregated country-level performance information

## Technical Requirements

- Use TypeScript with explicit types
- Build reusable functions
- Handle empty arrays and invalid inputs
- Avoid modifying original arrays
- Keep calculations and data processing separate from user interfaces
- Follow the business rules defined in BRIEFING.md
